About the Conference

Equipping Leaders to
Strengthen the Church

Open to Assistant Life Group Leaders (ALGLs) and above only. Join us for an enriching experience that promises equipping, growth, inspiration, and fellowship.

REACH UP

Reaching UP to God
  • Through developing passionate prayer, worship and relationship with God personally and corporately.
  • Through encountering and experiencing the love of Jesus Christ.

REACH IN

Reaching IN to develop covenantal relationships
  • Through fellowship to develop loving and intimate relationships and sharing of burdens.
  • Through close, loving, authentic relationships with each other.
  • By praying for each other, for their leaders and for the church.
  • By sharing burdens and blessings with each other.

REACH DEEP

Reaching DEEP to make disciples
  • By building strong biblical people through life-changing sharing, teaching, training, accountability, and discipling.
  • By training the members spiritually and practically.

REACH OUT

Reaching OUT to souls in need
  • Through effective witnessing, outreach, evangelism and missions.
  • By bringing pre-believers friends and family members.
  • By encouraging each other and pre-believers with testimonies.
